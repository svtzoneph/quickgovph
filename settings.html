Help & FAQs

Frequently asked questions about QuickGov PH.

How long does a Barangay Clearance take?
Depending on your local barangay, processing usually takes 1 to 2 working days. You will see the status change to "Ready for Pickup" in your 'My Requests' tab. Ensure your ID documents are clear and valid to avoid delays.
What Valid IDs are accepted?
We accept primary government-issued IDs such as National ID (Philsys), Passport, Driver's License, UMID, Voter's ID, and Postal ID. Secondary IDs like Company ID or School ID might require supplementary documents.
How does the SOS feature work?
When you trigger the SOS in the Residency Map, your exact GPS coordinates and profile information are instantly broadcasted to the Admin Dashboard of your local LGU for immediate response. A siren alert will trigger on the local command center.
How can I track my document request?
You can track the real-time status of your documents under the 'My Requests' tab. The tracker updates from "Pending" to "On Process" and finally "Ready for Pickup". You can tap "Confirm Receipt" once you obtain your physical copy.
Is my personal information secure?
Yes, we comply strictly with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. All submitted data is encrypted, and only authorized Local Government Unit (LGU) personnel have access to your submitted identity documents.
What should I do if my address is not supported?
Currently, QuickGov PH is rolling out per municipality. If your area is out of coverage, please contact your local barangay captain or mayor's office to encourage them to join and integrate with the platform.

Privacy Notice

How we handle your data.

Data Collection

QuickGov PH collects your personal information (name, email, address, and uploaded valid IDs) solely for the purpose of verifying your identity and processing local government requests (e.g., Barangay Clearances, Indigency Certificates). We also collect precise GPS location data exclusively when you interact with the Residency Map or trigger an Emergency SOS.

Data Protection & Security

  • All uploaded documents are securely processed in encrypted cloud servers and are only visible to authorized LGU personnel.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to third-party marketing entities.
  • Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard Secure Socket Layer (SSL) protocols.
  • Access to the administrative dashboard requires multi-factor authentication for government employees.

Data Retention

Your data is kept only as long as necessary to fulfill your requests or as required by Philippine law. Once a document request is completed, attached identification files are securely archived or purged according to the strict data retention policy of your specific Local Government Unit.

Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data from our systems. You can also opt-out of non-essential notifications via the Notifications tab. For deeper data concerns, please contact your local barangay admin or our Data Protection Officer.

Terms of Service

Guidelines for using the QuickGov PH Portal.

Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Any documents requested or SOS alerts triggered under your account are presumed to be authorized by you. You must provide accurate, truthful, and up-to-date information during registration and document requests.

Prohibited Activities

  • Submitting fake, forged, or altered identification documents to deceive local authorities.
  • Triggering false or prank Emergency SOS alerts (subject to severe local penalties and legal action).
  • Attempting to hack, overload, reverse-engineer, or disrupt the QuickGov PH servers and databases.
  • Using the platform for any illegal activities, harassment, or unauthorized commercial purposes.

Service Availability

We strive for 24/7 platform availability to ensure you always have access to local services. However, scheduled maintenance, system upgrades, and localized network outages may occur. The Emergency SOS feature relies heavily on your active internet or mobile data connection; always keep traditional national hotlines (911, 117) in mind as reliable backups.

Limitation of Liability

While QuickGov PH provides the digital infrastructure to connect you with your government, we are not liable for delays in emergency response, document processing rejections, or operational constraints caused directly by your Local Government Unit or sudden internet service interruptions.

About QuickGov PH

Digitalizing Local Governance.

Our Mission

QuickGov PH was built to bridge the gap between citizens and their local government units. By providing a centralized digital portal, we aim to streamline document requests, enhance emergency response, and foster a transparent, connected community.

Our Vision

To create a unified, digital-first Philippines where vital local government services are easily accessible to every citizen, anytime, anywhere, eliminating long lines and bureaucratic delays.

Key Features

  • Document Requests: Fast, paperless transactions for clearances, permits, and certificates.
  • Residency Map: Intelligent geographic mapping of local facilities, weather, and hazard zones.
  • Emergency SOS: Direct lifeline broadcasting your exact location to your local authorities.
  • Real-time Tracking: Transparent status updates for all your governmental requests.

Commitment to Excellence

Built with the Filipino citizen in mind, QuickGov PH continuously evolves. We partner directly with Mayors, Barangay Captains, and MDRRMOs to ensure that the transition from manual to digital governance is seamless, transparent, and highly efficient.
