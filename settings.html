Help & FAQs

Frequently asked questions about QuickGov PH.

How long does a Barangay Clearance take?
Depending on your local barangay, processing usually takes 1 to 2 working days. You will see the status change to "Ready for Pickup" in your 'My Requests' tab.
What Valid IDs are accepted?
We accept primary government-issued IDs such as National ID (Philsys), Passport, Driver's License, UMID, Voter's ID, and Postal ID.
How does the SOS feature work?
When you trigger the SOS in the Residency Map, your exact GPS coordinates and profile information are instantly broadcasted to the Admin Dashboard of your local LGU for immediate response.

Privacy Notice

How we handle your data.

Data Collection

QuickGov PH collects your personal information (name, email, address, and uploaded valid IDs) solely for the purpose of verifying your identity and processing local government requests (e.g., Barangay Clearances, Indigency Certificates).

Data Protection

  • All uploaded documents are securely processed and are only visible to authorized LGU personnel.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to third-party entities.
  • Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.

Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data from our systems. Contact your local barangay admin for assistance.

Terms of Service

Guidelines for using the QuickGov PH Portal.

Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Any documents requested or SOS alerts triggered under your account are presumed to be authorized by you.

Prohibited Activities

  • Submitting fake, forged, or altered identification documents.
  • Triggering false Emergency SOS alerts (subject to local penalties).
  • Using the platform for any illegal activities or unauthorized commercial purposes.

Limitation of Liability

While QuickGov PH ensures maximum uptime, we are not liable for delays in emergency response or document processing caused by local government operational constraints or internet service interruptions.

About QuickGov PH

Digitalizing Local Governance.

Our Mission

QuickGov PH was built to bridge the gap between citizens and their local government units. By providing a centralized digital portal, we aim to streamline document requests, enhance emergency response, and foster a transparent community.

Features

  • Document Requests: Fast and paperless transactions.
  • Residency Map: Intelligent geographic mapping of facilities and hazards.
  • Emergency SOS: Direct lifeline to your local authorities.
